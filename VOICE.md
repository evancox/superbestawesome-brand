# superbestawesome voice

How every superbestawesome product talks. The library is the full-volume version; the journal
says the same things more quietly.

## The name

- Always `superbestawesome`: one word, all lowercase, even at the start of a sentence.
- Never with a period after it: not "superbestawesome.", "sba." or "s.".
- Product names are lowercase too and follow the wordmark: superbestawesome **journal**.
  In running text: "the journal", "the library".

## How it sounds

- **Plain words, short sentences.** "Nothing uploaded yet." not "You haven't uploaded any content."
- **Say what happens, not what the feature is called.** "Nobody else can watch until processing
  finishes." beats "Processing status: pending."
- **Warm, never cute.** One light touch per screen at most. No exclamation marks in errors.
- **Speak to one person.** "you" and "your"; "we" only when superbestawesome itself acts.
- **Privacy stated, not sold.** "Only you can see these." Never "your data is safe with us".
- **Sentence case everywhere**: buttons, titles, menus. Buttons are verbs: "Add", "Save", "Invite someone".
- **Optional is marked, required isn't.** "Description (optional)".
- **Empty states point forward.** "Nothing yet. Add something above."
- **Errors say what to do next.** "That link has expired. Ask for a new one."
- **States confirm briefly:** "Saving…", "Saved ✓", "Copied ✓".

## The calm tone (journal)

- Fewer words again. The person may be tired, upset, or capturing one-handed.
- No streak shaming, no "you missed", no nagging. A skipped day is just a day.
- Reflect, don't advise: "You mentioned sleep four times this week." not "You should sleep more."
- Questions are open and skippable: "Anything else from today?"
